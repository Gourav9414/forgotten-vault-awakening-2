How to playClusters: 5+ matching sigils connected up/down/left/right can win. Diagonals do not connect.
Base payout per 5-symbol cluster: Lunar Pearl 0.21× bet; Astral Star 0.252×; Oracle Eye 0.315×; Void Crystal 0.42×; Eclipse Crown 0.525×; Vault Key 0.63×; Wild-only cluster 0.21×. Payout scales with cluster size; rune and feature multipliers apply separately.
Cascades: winning tiles vanish; tiles fall and new sigils enter.
Wild sigil: a lightning Wild can appear naturally (4% per tile) and substitutes for a regular symbol in a cluster. Connected groups of 5+ Wilds also win by themselves; Wilds are counted as part of the connected cluster, including patterns such as 3 Wilds + 2 matching symbols or 4 Wilds + 1 matching symbol, provided all cells connect orthogonally. Wild groups can participate in multiple different symbol clusters in the same cascade; identical clusters are deduplicated, and each candidate cluster pays once. Zap Wild: when a board has no connection, there is a 5% chance per eligible spin for a visible lightning Wild to appear and complete a 5+ connection. It can trigger at most once per spin. This rescue feature is disclosed and is part of the prototype math, which is not RTP-verified.
Shining multiplier runes: each winning hit advances the multiplier by one level. A winning cluster gets multiplied once if at least one shining rune is directly adjacent to it; multiple adjacent runes never stack the multiplier.
Guardian bonus: 4 Guardians trigger Normal at 4 (3 starting rounds), Higher at 5 (5 rounds), Mythic at 6–7 (6 rounds), and Epic at 8+ (8 rounds). In bonus rounds, 4 Guardians award +3 rounds, 5 award +5, 6–7 award +6, and 8+ award +8. A higher tier upgrades and restarts the feature while retaining prior winnings. Starting multipliers: Normal ×2, Higher ×4, Mythic ×8. Epic ladder: ×50 → ×100 → ×200 → ×300 → ×500 → ×750 → ×1,000. The ladder advances after 2 winning cascades, then requires 3, then 4, and so on; progress carries across retriggers and resets on tier upgrade. A winning cluster receives the active multiplier only when a shining rune is directly adjacent. Multiple adjacent runes never stack. Prototype only; RTP must be recalibrated and validated after these mechanic changes.
